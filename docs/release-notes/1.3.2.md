A maintenance release with one fix: Game & Wario's Gamer music also converts at 140 and 200 BPM. Every other file converts as in 1.3.1.

## Downloads

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `cafesf2-1.3.2-win-x64.zip` | Windows x64 executable, `README.md`, `LICENSE` and `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` |

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. No additional runtime DLLs are required. Drop one or more `.bfsar`, `.baa` or `.aaf` files onto `cafesf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `cafesf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Cafesf2.app`. Drop archives onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## Changes

- **Game & Wario.** In the game Gamer, the music follows the BPM of the game in play, and a session starts at 120, 140 or 200 BPM. Each of Gamer's 32 pieces now also converts as two arrangements, such as `BGM_NI_GAME_FISH1 140 BPM`. They play the piece at 7/6 of the tempo and 2 semitones higher, or at 5/3 of the tempo and 8 semitones higher. The game sets the same tempo ratio and pitch. The files of the piece itself still play at 120 BPM.
- **Reading an archive.** cafesf2 reads each sound archive and its group files with ntdlib's frame. The messages and the files stay the same.
- **The README.** The README now says how `--loops N` counts the passes before the loop, and that a dot or a space at the end of a file name becomes `_`. Its wording and the wording of the earlier release notes are clearer.
- **Third-party notices.** `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` now lists the SHA-1 code from xsflib and the sequence starts from ntdlib.
- **The libraries.** cafesf2 now builds on seqlib 1.5.0, xsflib 1.12.0 and ntdlib 7.0.0.

## Known limitations

As in 1.3.1, with one more. Some Gamer sessions in Game & Wario change BPM from game to game. Those later BPMs aren't converted. See "JAudio games", "SFZ files" and "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
