A feature release: cafesf2 converts the sound effects of The Wind Waker HD. Notes that the game sends through the DSP's automatic mixer now play at half the level of the others. Sound archives convert as in 1.1.2.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. No additional runtime DLLs are required. Drop one or more `.bfsar`, `.baa` or `.aaf` files onto `cafesf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `cafesf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Cafesf2.app`. Drop archives onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## New

- **The Wind Waker HD's sound effects.** The first JAudio plays all of a game's sound effects in one sequence. The sound library starts an effect by writing ports of one of that sequence's slots. cafesf2 used to leave the sequence out. cafesf2 now converts each effect as that sequence plays it when the game starts the effect. The effects come last, in the sound table's order. An effect that the game doesn't name becomes `se<category>-<index>`, such as `se4-0123`. An effect plays at its volume in the sound table times the volume of its category. Each effect also plays at the pitch that the table gives it. The MIDI file holds that pitch as a pitch bend. The README's new section "The first JAudio's sound effects" describes the effects.

## Fixes

- **The DSP's automatic mixer.** A track of the first JAudio can send its notes through the DSP's automatic mixer. That mixer sets their gains from their volume and pan. cafesf2 now plays those notes at half the level of the others. That level approximates the game's mix. The Wind Waker HD's sound effects and some of its music use the mixer. That music used to play too loudly against the rest.
- **The warning for missing group files.** cafesf2 looks for the group files that an archive keeps outside it: beside the archive, in a `userManagementFiles` folder and in the `.sarc` files beside the archive. When cafesf2 finds none of the groups, the warning now names the `.sarc` files too.

## Other changes

- **The libraries.** cafesf2 now builds on ntdlib 1.12.0 and xsflib 1.6.0, with seqlib 1.3.0.

## Known limitations

As in 1.1.2, apart from the changes above. The Wind Waker HD's code passes values with some sound effects, such as the material of the ground under a footstep. cafesf2 passes 0 for each. Some effects then play nothing. A game keeps some effects going by starting them again and again. cafesf2 plays each effect from one start: to its end, or with loop markers where it loops. Notes on the DSP's automatic mixer play at half the level of the others. That level approximates the game's mix. See "JAudio games", "SFZ files" and "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
