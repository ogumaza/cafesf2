A release with fixes to the conversion of JAudio games and to SFZ files with key groups. The sound effects of Twilight Princess HD and The Wind Waker HD now play their notes on the DSP's built-in oscillators. In both games, a note now ends where the game's volume falls silent. Sound archives convert to the same MIDI files and SoundFonts as in 1.2.0.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. No additional runtime DLLs are required. Drop one or more `.bfsar`, `.baa` or `.aaf` files onto `cafesf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `cafesf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Cafesf2.app`. Drop archives onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## Fixes

- **The DSP's built-in oscillators.** JAudio plays programs 0xf0 and up on the DSP's built-in oscillators. cafesf2 used to leave every note on them out. The square wave, the sawtooth, the pulse of 25% duty, the triangle and the sine now become looping samples of one cycle, one for each program. Each preset of such a program has one zone, for every key and velocity. The zone holds the oscillators' envelope: full volume until the release, then a fall to silence over about 17 ms. Sound effects of both games play such notes. Some of those effects used to convert with no notes. The DSP changes its noise and its ramp as it plays. No sample can hold them. cafesf2 still leaves out the notes on them and reports them.
- **Notes that fall silent.** A JAudio note's volume can fall to zero and hold there until the note's release. The game's channel is silent from there. The MIDI note used to go on to the release. The SoundFont's fitted decay could then ring on for seconds where the game was silent. The note now ends where its volume holds at zero and no release can raise it. Pieces of music and sound effects of both games change.
- **Key groups in SFZ files.** A note of a key group ends the group's other notes with the game's fade at release rate 126. The SFZ files used to leave sfizz to cut those notes in about 6 ms, its default. Each region with a key group now gets `off_mode=time` and an `off_time` of the game's fade. The SoundFonts stay the same. A SoundFont's exclusive class can't set that fade.
- **The report.** cafesf2 cuts a SoundFont's level where an instrument plays above full level. The report now names the cut only when it shows as 0.1 dB or more. It used to name cuts of 0.0 dB too.

## Other changes

- **The README.** The SFZ section names two more quirks of sfizz 1.2.3. sfizz pans a mono sample by the sin/cos law and then raises both sides by 3 dB. Every sample of the SFZ files is mono. sfizz therefore plays every region 3.0 dB above the game's level, at every pan. The balance of the mix stays the same. Lowering sfizz's volume by 3 dB gives the game's level. A key group in sfizz also never cuts a note of the same key as the new note. A key of a group struck again therefore leaves the earlier note playing in sfizz. The game fades that note out.
- **The libraries.** cafesf2 now builds on ntdlib 2.0.0, with xsflib 1.6.0 and seqlib 1.3.0.

## Known limitations

As in 1.2.0, apart from the changes above. cafesf2 leaves out the notes on the DSP's noise, its ramp and the other oscillators that no sample can hold. See "JAudio games", "SFZ files" and "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
