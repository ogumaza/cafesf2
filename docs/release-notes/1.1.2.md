A release that brings the SoundFonts and SFZ files in line with their specifications and with the players that most people use. MIDI files, samples and messages convert as in 1.1.1.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. No additional runtime DLLs are required. Drop one or more `.bfsar`, `.baa` or `.aaf` files onto `cafesf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `cafesf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Cafesf2.app`. Drop archives onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## Fixes

- **SoundFont attenuation.** FluidSynth, BASSMIDI and the E-mu chips apply 0.4 of a zone's initial attenuation. cafesf2 now writes 2.5 times the attenuation in the generator. Those players then play each zone at the game's level. The generator holds at most 1440 cB. Those players play that as 57.6 dB. A modulator without a source holds any attenuation beyond that. A player that follows the specification plays attenuated zones too quietly.
- **SoundFont pans.** The specification gives the default CC10 modulator an amount of 1000. The players play 500. Each instrument's global zone now holds a CC10 modulator with an amount of 500 in place of the default. A player that follows the specification then pans as the others do.
- **SFZ envelopes.** The SFZ documentation's decays and releases fall by a factor of e^-8 over their time. cafesf2 wrote them for sfizz's fall of e^-9. The SFZ files now give the times for the documentation's curve. sfizz 1.2.3 still falls by e^-9. In sfizz, decays and releases now end sooner than in the game.
- **SFZ opcodes in their documented ranges.** A velocity range that starts at 0 now starts at `lovel=1`. A velocity of 0 is a note-off. Bend ranges are whole cents. The filters' curves and amounts lie within the documented ranges. They give the same cutoffs and resonances as before. A region of a Wii U sound archive stops at the documented volume of 6 dB.

## Other changes

- **The README.** The notes on SoundFont players cover BASSMIDI 2.4.16 and FluidSynth's newer versions. They no longer say that the SoundFonts target FluidSynth. They give the two places where the SoundFonts depart from the specification: the zones' attenuation and the biquad low-pass's resonance. The SFZ section names sfizz 1.2 as the player tested. It lists the opcodes whose values can lie outside the documented ranges. It also describes a third quirk of sfizz 1.2.3.
- **The libraries.** cafesf2 now builds on seqlib 1.3.0, xsflib 1.5.0 and ntdlib 1.11.0.

## Known limitations

As in 1.1.1, apart from the fixes above. See "JAudio games", "SFZ files" and "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
