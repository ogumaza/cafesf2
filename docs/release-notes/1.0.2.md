A release that comes closer to the game's tone and level. AX darkens a note that plays more than one sample of its wave per output sample. Such notes now play darker in the SoundFonts and SFZ files too. In the SoundFonts, strongly filtered notes no longer play several decibels too loud. Convert an archive again to get the corrected files.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. No additional runtime DLLs are required. Drop one or more `.bfsar` files onto `cafesf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `cafesf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each archive.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Cafesf2.app`. Drop archives onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## New

- **A new report line.** The SoundFont leaves the new resampling low-pass out of a preset whose filter changes during notes. cafesf2 reports the notes above a rate of 1 that such a preset plays. The SFZ files keep the low-pass.
- **FluidSynth's level.** The README explains a difference in FluidSynth's level. FluidSynth plays every note 1.5 dB louder than the SoundFont specifies. Multiplying FluidSynth's gain by 0.84 gives the specified level.

## Fixes

- **AX's resampling.** AX resamples each voice through one of three sets of filters. The voice's rate decides which set when the voice starts. The rate is the wave's sample rate times the pitch ratio, over AX's 32 kHz. Above a rate of 1, the filters cut the highest frequencies. Above 4/3, they cut more of them. cafesf2 now splits each zone and region at the keys where the rate passes 1 and 4/3. The upper parts get a low-pass whose cutoff tracks the key. Such notes used to play brighter than in the game.
- **The track low-pass at low cutoffs.** The SoundFont's two-pole low-pass could play a strongly filtered note several decibels louder than the game's one-pole filter does. Where the table's cutoff misses the game's level by more than 1 dB, a zone now takes the cutoff at which FluidSynth passes as much of its wave as the game does. The SFZ files still play the game's filter.

## Known limitations

As in 1.0.1, apart from the fixes above. A zone's resampling low-pass follows the rate at the zone's key. A note that starts with its bend, sweep or LFO across 1 or 4/3 therefore plays through its key's low-pass. See "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
