A feature release: cafesf2 starts the sequences of seven games' sound archives with the values and the tracks that the games' code gives them, and converts their other states as arrangements. The music of The Wind Waker HD and Twilight Princess HD converts in the states that the games play it in. Twilight Princess HD gets its echo and the volumes of its categories of sound effects. Notes whose pitch passes AX's rate limit now play at the limit, as in the game.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. No additional runtime DLLs are required. Drop one or more `.bfsar`, `.baa` or `.aaf` files onto `cafesf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `cafesf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Cafesf2.app`. Drop archives onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## New

- **Profiles of sound archives.** cafesf2 has profiles for the sound archives of six games, all from the USA: Animal Crossing: amiibo Festival, Mario Party 10, Nintendo Land, Super Mario 3D World, New Super Mario Bros. U and Wii Party U (Rev 1). It finds an archive's profile by the archive's contents. In those archives, a sequence that waits for the game's variables starts with the values that the game's code gives it, where a reading of the code found them. Other values that change what the sequence plays convert as arrangements. Each arrangement is one more set of files after the sequence, named after it with each variable and its value added, such as `SQ_SE_MG0B05_TANK_L_AXEL_15_1`. Where the game computes a value as it plays, such as a lift's speed, or where no reading found it, the sequence starts with -1 as before, and its arrangements hold values that change what it plays. A profile's values replace those of `--var`. Many of these sequences played nothing before.
- **Kirby and the Rainbow Curse's stage select.** The game plays one piece, `BGM_SEQ_MENU_INTERACTIVE`, with one to three of its tracks, picked by the view: the area view, a world's list of stages, or a world's boss stage. The piece's files used to play every track at once. They now play the area view's track alone. Each of the 14 other states converts as an arrangement named after the state, such as `BGM_SEQ_MENU_INTERACTIVE world 3 boss`. A track that a state turns down plays at CC11 0 from the start. Its notes stay in the MIDI file.
- **The Wind Waker HD's music.** The game's sound layer mutes some tracks of 14 pieces as it starts them. The game's state picks the tracks, such as the boat's sail or an enemy near Link. Some of the game's IDs start a piece in another arrangement. cafesf2 converts each piece as the game starts it, in the state that the sound layer starts in: the boat's sail down, Link off the boat, his sword sheathed and no enemy near. It also converts each other arrangement, named after the piece and the game's ID or the state, such as `JA_BGM_KAMI_TOWER_80000125` or `JA_BGM_SEA_SAILING`. While some pieces play, the game writes a status to their port 9, such as the phase of a boss fight. Such a piece converts once more for each value that changes what it plays, such as `JA_BGM_P_GANON_1_PORT9_2`. The battle music also converts with 1 on port 11, as when Link hits an enemy. cafesf2 recognizes the start-up archives of the USA and Japanese releases.
- **Twilight Princess HD's music.** As the game starts five pieces, it mutes some of their tracks. cafesf2 now leaves out the muted tracks' notes. Hyrule Field's day music holds two arrangements: `Z2BGM_FIELD_LINK_DAY` plays while Link walks, and `Z2BGM_FIELD_LINK_DAY_riding` while he rides. Later, the game's scenes, fights and other code change the tracks of 17 pieces, some of them to part of their volume. cafesf2 converts each later state as one more file of the piece, named with the status that the game gives the piece, such as `Z2BGM_TOAL_VILLEGE_status1`. The battle music's states follow the fight: `_near`, `_searched`, `_near_searched` and `_port9_1`. The boss and minigame pieces whose actors write a status once the piece plays convert with that status after the first tick. Hyrule Field's day music also follows the field's code: what Link does, the enemies near him and the time of day. cafesf2 converts each way through the piece as one more file in each arrangement.
- **Twilight Princess HD's echo and categories.** The game sets up an echo at start-up. HD's echo repeats every 152 ms. A file that sends to the echo now ends when the echo would fall silent. The echo itself isn't in the file. The game also gives its categories of sound effects their volumes at start-up. Category 1, Link's voice, keeps the volume of the sound table. The others play at 120/127 of it. cafesf2 now does the same.

## Fixes

- **AX's rate limit.** AX plays a voice at a rate of 8 at most: 8 samples of its wave for each sample of the 32 kHz mix. A note whose key starts past the limit and whose pitch holds still now gets zones and regions that play at 8, as in the game. Such notes used to play up to several semitones sharp. A note that a bend, a sweep or an LFO moves still plays above the limit where the game holds it there.
- **JAudio's track filters.** A music track's filters now play through two zones of each region: one unfiltered and one low-passed. They used to be left out. The track filters of sound effects and the filters that change from note to note are still left out.
- **Silent endings.** A sequence that ends but runs on through silent waits for more than 10 s after its last sound now ends where its sound dies away.
- **Loops that wait for the game.** A loop that repeats until a variable that only the game sets says otherwise could stop on a silent pass. It now loops as the game plays it while the variable keeps its value.

## Other changes

- **The README.** The SFZ section names another quirk of sfizz 1.2.3: it makes each decay and release last at least 6 ms. The README also describes AX's rate limit, the profiles and the games' states.
- **The libraries.** cafesf2 now builds on ntdlib 3.2.0 and xsflib 1.8.0, with seqlib 1.3.0.

## Known limitations

As in 1.2.1, apart from the changes above. The game moves a piece of music into a new state gradually. cafesf2 plays each state from the start and leaves out the states in between. A track that the game brings in while a fight goes on comes in over 5 or 10 frames in the game, and plays from the start in the files. See "JAudio games", "SFZ files" and "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
